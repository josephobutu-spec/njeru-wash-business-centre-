## About

Njeru WASH Business Centre provides affordable, high-quality water, sanitation and hygiene solutions to households, institutions and businesses in the Njeru area and surrounding communities. We supply products, provide installation and repair services, and offer consultancy and training on WASH best practices.

Our mission is to improve public health and dignity by making safe water, clean sanitation, and proper hygiene accessible to everyone.

## Our Services

- Water supply solutions
  - Borehole drilling (partners/contractors)
  - Water storage tanks and tanks installation
  - Water pump supply, installation and maintenance
- Sanitation services
  - VIP latrine construction
  - Septic tank emptying and maintenance
  - Improved toilet systems (ecosan, pour-flush)
- Hygiene promotion and training
  - Community and school hygiene training
  - Handwashing stations (manufacture / installation)
- On-site repair and maintenance
  - Leak repair, pump servicing, pipe replacement
- Consultancy and assessments
  - Water quality testing referrals
  - WASH needs assessments for organizations

## Products

- Handwashing stations (tippy taps, metal/wood stands)
- Ceramic and plastic water storage tanks (various capacities)
- Submersible and surface pumps (electric & solar-ready)
- Pipes, fittings and valves
- Water treatment chemicals and simple filtration kits
- Hygiene supplies (soap, sanitizers, menstrual hygiene products)

(Inventory and catalog are managed separately — contact us for current availability and pricing.)

## How to Order / Get Services

1. Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email (see Contact below) with a short description of what you need.
2. We will schedule a site visit or phone consultation to assess needs and provide a quote.
3. Upon agreement, we schedule delivery/installation and provide estimated timelines.
4. We offer follow-up maintenance and can set up periodic service contracts for institutions.

Payment methods: Cash, mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money), and bank transfer where applicable.
